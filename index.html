How to Use Kana

Your AI assistant is ready. Here is the fastest way to get comfortable using Kana for everyday help, research, writing, reminders, and more.

Start with Telegram See example prompts

01 · Getting Started with Telegram

Message your bot like you would message a person

1. Open your Telegram link

Kana lives in Telegram first. Tap the link you received during setup and open the bot chat. If Telegram asks you to confirm, just continue into the conversation.

2. Complete the pairing flow

The first message may guide you through a quick pairing or confirmation step. Once connected, that chat becomes your direct line to Kana.

3. Send your first message

Keep it simple. Try: “Hey Kana, help me plan my week.” You do not need special commands. Natural language works best.

First message idea

Telegram chat

Hey Kana — I just got set up. Can you help me organize my priorities for this week?

  • • Short messages are fine
  • • Long detailed requests are also fine
  • • You can ask follow-up questions anytime

03 · Tips & Tricks

Use voice when typing feels slow

Send voice notes in Telegram when you are walking, driving, or thinking out loud. Kana can still help from a casual spoken prompt.

Ask follow-ups naturally

You do not need to restate everything. After Kana replies, just say things like “make it shorter,” “turn this into bullet points,” or “do the same for marketing tools.”

Set reminders early

Kana is especially useful when you offload memory. Try reminders for follow-ups, deadlines, calls, and ideas you do not want to lose.
